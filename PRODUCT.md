# Editor Desk

A private, offline media preparation and handoff tool for a production owner and a human editor. It catalogs client camera originals, separate recordings, and prepared exports without touching the originals. Approved Trudent work is the reference, not a promise that one lighting-specific grade suits every new scene.

The workflow is import, find a recording, review sync and coverage, choose the camera profile and framing, preview the grade, prepare a clip, then hand it to the human editor for story, captions, music and branding. Unconfirmed matches and camera-audio fallbacks remain explicit. The application and actual media live together in a portable folder; no cloud, account or paid API is required.
